StatementFlow is a browser-based tool that converts South African bank statement PDFs into clean, structured CSV data. No uploads, no accounts, no servers.

Currently in active development. Live and free to use at https://lethuxololanga.github.io/statement-convert/. All processing happens entirely in your browser; your financial data never leaves your device.

Contributions are welcome. More banks coming soon.
